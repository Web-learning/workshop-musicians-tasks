# workshop-musicians-tasks
A collection of tasks for musicians to complete so that they get onto the knowledge graph
* Have we agreed on an [agenda](https://mensuel.framapad.org/p/Musicians)?
* Have you read my blog post about connected musicians. Do not stress about comprehension. 
* Is your venue listed on the [concerts venue map](data/concerts.geojson)?
* Do you have a website? Social Media Accounts? Published Articles
* Do you have other object identifiers?
** Spotify (P1902)?
** Amazon Music artist ID (P6276)?
* Lets get your pics onto Wikimedia Commons and your metadata onto Wikidata
No deletions? Good. You are probably notable enough to move onto English Wikipedia (for the brave)
